# When the Russia-Ukraine War Could Be Finished: Status as of November 2026

## Summary
As of November 15, 2026, there is no ceasefire or peace agreement in Ukraine, and the latest Abu Dhabi talks ended without a breakthrough. Forecasts vary, with Metaculus giving about 15% odds of a ceasefire by end-2026 and analysts generally expecting no settlement this year. The sources do not support a firm end date; the main obstacles are Donbas territory and security guarantees.

## Findings
1. As of mid-November 2026 there is no general ceasefire or peace agreement; US-brokered trilateral talks resumed in Abu Dhabi on Nov 10-11 with no breakthrough announced, and territory (Donbas) and security guarantees remain the main obstacles. (https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/11/10/russia-ukraine-begin-us-backed-peace-talks-abu-dhabi, https://www.outlookindia.com/international/russia-ukraine-peace-talks-2026-abu-dhabi-donbas-nato-security-guarantees)
2. Zelensky reported that Putin rejected a bilateral energy ceasefire, and US envoys agreed to fly to Putin to discuss it; the outcome of that trip is not yet reported. (https://kyivpost.com/post/90119)
3. Only short, localized truces have occurred, including a 32-hour Orthodox Easter truce in April 2026 and a six-day Kherson-region humanitarian ceasefire that expired around Nov 1-2 without extension. (https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/1/ukraine-evacuates-civilians-in-kherson-region-as-localised-ceasefire-ends, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Russo-Ukrainian_truce)
4. Forecasts differ: Metaculus puts the chance of a ceasefire by end-2026 at about 15%, with a most likely first lasting ceasefire around November 2027, while some analysts suggest 2029 and RFE/RL analysts expect the war will not end in 2026. (https://ukranews.com/en/news/1165616-not-this-year-international-analysts-assess-chances-of-ending-war-in-ukraine, https://www.rferl.org/a/russia-invasion-ukraine-war-future-2026/33644095.html, https://www.csis.org/analysis/russias-war-ukraine-next-chapter)
5. Territory is the central blocker: Russia demands full control of Donbas, while Ukraine insists on dividing territory along current front lines; security guarantees are the key precondition, with Russia rejecting European troops in Ukraine and Europe favoring a ceasefire first. (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/ukraine-russia-peace-talks-zelenskyy-putin.html, https://www.kcra.com/article/us-led-peace-talks-russia-ukraine-security-guarantees/74373247)
