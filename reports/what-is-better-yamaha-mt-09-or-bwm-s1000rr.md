# Yamaha MT-09 vs BMW S1000RR: 2026 Comparison

## Summary
The BMW S1000RR is a far more powerful, track-focused superbike at a higher price, while the Yamaha MT-09 is a lighter, cheaper, more comfortable street naked. For most street riders the MT-09 is the practical choice, and the S1000RR suits those who want track performance. Price and horsepower figures vary between sources, and reliability and running costs could not be verified.

## Findings
1. The 2026 Yamaha MT-09 has an 890cc inline-three rated around 117-119 hp and 93 Nm, weighing about 193 kg wet; sources differ slightly on the power figure due to rounding. (https://www.torquepedia.com/yamaha/mt09/2026, https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2026/2026-yamaha-mt09)
2. The MT-09 lists at about $10,899 MSRP in the US, plus roughly $700 destination, while the MT-09 SP is $12,699; the destination fee rests on a single dealer listing. (https://www.motorcycle.com/specs/yamaha/standard/2026/mt/09/detail.html, https://www.torquepedia.com/yamaha/mt09sp/2026)
3. The 2026 BMW S 1000 RR uses a 999cc inline-four rated at about 205 hp and 113 Nm, weighing about 198 kg wet, with Race ABS, a quickshifter, and Riding Modes Pro as standard; one site claims 212 hp for the M Package, which is unverified. (https://www.motorcycle.com/specs/bmw/sport/2026/s/1000-rr/detail.html, https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2026/2026-bmw-s1000rr)
4. The S1000RR costs more and makes far more power, but sources disagree on its price: about $19,755 MSRP in one listing and $19,895 in another, with a European price of about EUR 21,500. (https://www.torquepedia.com/bmw/s1000rr/2026, https://carsfrenzy.net/2026-bmw-s1000rr-price)
5. The MT-09 is the more comfortable, practical everyday street bike, with a lower 825 mm seat and a relaxed upright riding position, while the S1000RR is a race-oriented sport bike suited to track use. (https://www.1000ps.com/en-us/comparison/362004/yamaha-mt-09-2018-vs-bmw-s-1000-rr-2024, https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2026/2026-bmw-s1000rr)
